Cookies and browser storage

Cookies are small pieces of information a website can ask your browser to store and send with later requests. Local storage and session storage are other ways of saving information on your device. These differ from the network requests needed to load pages, images, and fonts.

The current site

The current SOPliance website code does not set cookies, write to local storage or session storage, or include analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or embedded social-media widgets. The job-title search works in the page without saving your search as a browser preference. There are no optional tracking categories to enable or disable in this version.

Hosting and fonts

GitHub Pages hosts this preview and logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes. The site also requests typefaces from Google Fonts. A network request can expose information to a provider without using a cookie. See our Privacy Policy, GitHub Pages information, and Google Fonts privacy information.

External services

The LinkedIn link takes you to LinkedIn and does not embed a feed here. External sites may use cookies under their own policies. Contact and application links open your email application, whose storage and privacy settings are managed by that application and its provider.

Your browser controls

Manage cookies and site data in your browser settings, including deleting stored data or blocking cookie categories. Blocking cookies does not stop all network requests or hosting security logs. Extensions and other websites can also affect the cookies shown in your browser.

Changes and questions

This policy must be reviewed before adding analytics, advertising, embedded content, saved preferences, or a new application platform. Any required consent controls must be in place before optional tracking is activated. For questions, email info@sopliance.com.
