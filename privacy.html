SOPLIANCE WEBSITE POLICIES

Privacy Policy

Draft for company review · Updated October 24, 2026

How the current website and email recruitment process handle information.

TermsPrivacyCookies

Scope and contact

SOPliance, Inc. is based in Florida. This draft covers the current website and information you choose to email about services or a specific advertised position. Send privacy questions to info@sopliance.com.

Information you provide

An inquiry may include your name, email address, phone number, organization, service needs, and correspondence. A role-specific application may also include your résumé, work history, education, qualifications, and attachments. The website has no application upload form or user accounts. Clicking an email link does not automatically send a message.

How information is used

Service inquiries are used to respond to your request and discuss your needs. Application information is used to review suitability for the specific role, communicate with you, and support recruitment or placement with client organizations. Identify the position in your application. Do not include Social Security numbers, banking information, identity documents, or medical details in an initial email.

Sharing with client organizations

SOPliance shares applicants' résumés with client organizations for recruitment and placement. A client may handle the information under its own recruitment processes and privacy notice. Contact SOPliance with questions about which client will receive your application, or before applying if you need to discuss a restriction on sharing.

Hosting and external providers

GitHub Pages hosts this preview. GitHub states that visitor IP addresses are logged for security purposes, including for visitors who are not signed in. See GitHub Pages information and the GitHub Privacy Statement. Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which requires browser connections to Google servers and transmits network information such as an IP address. See Google Fonts privacy information. Email correspondence passes through the email providers used by you and SOPliance. Providers may process information outside your country.

Cookies and tracking

The current website code does not set cookies, use local or session storage, or include analytics scripts or advertising pixels. See the Cookie Policy for details about browser storage and network requests.

Retention and privacy requests

The retention period for application and correspondence records is awaiting company confirmation before this policy is finalized. To ask about a submitted application, correct information, request deletion, or discuss restrictions on use, email info@sopliance.com. Identify the position and email address used to apply. Do not attach sensitive identity documents to your initial request. Available rights and exceptions depend on applicable law; some records may need to be kept for legal obligations.

Security and updates

Email and internet transmission cannot be guaranteed completely secure. Limit an initial application to information relevant to the position. This notice will be reviewed when hosting, recruitment processes, or website features change. The date above identifies the latest draft update.
